01

THE PROMISE

Official orientation

One record.
Every kind of potential.

Traditional examinations were retired in 2042. LearnScore now recognises abilities that a single test could miss. Since 2048, learning records have been linked to courses, scholarships, university admission and early career placement. LearnScore is a customized learning system that combines humans and AI. It gives students individualized learning plans that caters to their goals. It will also customize their daily learning plan so that no two students' curriculum look the same.

Every learner has a permanent account for memory, language, creativity, teamwork, attention, care work, emotional stability and community contribution. Scores may be challenged, but classroom staff cannot edit them.

AI tutors combine lessons, conversations, activity records and daily health scans to recommend each learner’s safest development path. Approved pathways receive priority access to public education. Simultaneously, there are human teachers and counselor that work alongside students for furthered help and development.

OFFICIAL NOTICE 7.2 LearnScore does not punish students.

It predicts risk so opportunity can be distributed fairly.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Final reflection

Key Takeaway

LearnScore touches on the topics of AI integration, scoring systems, and data privacy, almost in a dystopian way. While LearnScore improves the issue of a one-size-fits-all education system we have today, there’s a fine balance between how much AI to use for personalized learning. In the future, these technologies may exist, but it is crucial for people to have their sense of self, drive, and passion so they are not outshined by AI.
