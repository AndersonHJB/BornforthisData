THE PROMISE

Official orientation

One record.
Every kind of potential.

Traditional final examinations were retired in 2042. LearnScore now recognises abilities that a single test could miss. Since 2048, learning records have been linked to courses, scholarships, university admission and early career placement.

Every learner has a permanent account for memory, language, creativity, teamwork, attention, care work, emotional stability and community contribution. Scores may be challenged, but classroom staff cannot edit them.

AI tutors combine lessons, conversations, activity records and daily health scans to recommend each learner’s safest development path. Approved pathways receive priority access to public education.
