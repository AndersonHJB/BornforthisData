LEARNSCORE 2055 — MATERIALS AND PROVENANCE
===========================================

CONCEPT AND NARRATIVE
---------------------
The project extends the student's first assignment, “The Measured Child 2055”. LearnScore is presented as the national portal for that assignment's Learning Credit System. The interactive narrative was edited and structured from the supplied manuscript “文稿.md”.

DISPLAY COPIES
--------------
The five files ending in -display.jpg are mechanically cropped copies of the supplied drawings. Only excess white space was removed; no content was generated or altered. The uncropped originals remain in this folder and open through each “Inspect full artefact” control.

ORIGINAL MEDIA CREATED FOR THIS SITE
------------------------------------
- video.mp4: assembled locally from the five supplied drawings using deterministic pan, zoom and crossfade animation. No generative AI and no stock footage were used.
- learnscore-ambient.mp3: built from procedural sine tones and filtered noise. No third-party recording or music was used.
- Interface notification tones: synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- student-studio-paper-stage.png: generated specifically for this interface as a text-free decorative paper background. It contains no third-party photography or identifying information.

EXTERNAL MATERIALS
------------------
- Six interface icons are unmodified regular-weight SVG assets from Phosphor Icons, distributed under the MIT License. A copy of the license is included at assets/icons/PHOSPHOR-LICENSE.txt.
- The finished website has no runtime network dependencies, analytics or external APIs. All assets are stored locally.
