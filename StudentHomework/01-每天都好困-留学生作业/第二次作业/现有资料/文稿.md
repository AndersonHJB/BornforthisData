For a long time in the past, students relied on a few fixed methods to assess a student's abilities. However, the LearnScore website has completely changed all of this. By the year 2055, every learner will have a permanent account, and this account will continuously record the student's academic progress, creativity, teamwork, language skills, care work, attention, emotional health, and community contributions.

The launch of the LearnScore website is actually aimed at making education more personalized, flexible and fair. Students no longer study solely for final exams, but accumulate learning points throughout their lives. 
The methods for obtaining points will also vary. Students can earn creativity points by designing projects, care points by taking care of family members, cooperation points by helping the team complete tasks, and community points through volunteer work. 
At the same time, in the classroom, AI tutors will record your conversations, learning activities and daily health checks. Then, based on the students' abilities, they will recommend suitable courses, scholarships and career development directions for each student. 
LearnScore guarantees that every ability and talent of students will be recognized by the system. Students who are not good at certain subjects and have poor grades can still gain recognition through practical skills, communication skills, leadership abilities, or caregiving work. 
However, the data recorded by the system cannot be changed. At the same time, if students have low participation, unstable attention, or multiple emotional warnings, it may reduce the likelihood of them obtaining future opportunities. 
System Notification

LearnScore does not penalize students. It is only responsible for predicting risks. 
The Hidden Cost of LearnScore
This system, as explained by the country, can identify students' greater potential and can also uncover abilities that are often overlooked in traditional exams. It is said to make the education system more fair. However, in LearnScore, every class, every emotion, and every personal choice of a student will become an accompanying file for that student. 
The more important aspect of this system is that the recommendations made by the system are not suggestions but are mandatory. Therefore, whether a student can receive a scholarship, enter a specific course, or apply to a university is entirely determined by the system. So, it is a student who has the possibility of being interested in art, but the system, based on her memory, attention, and economic risk data, through big data analysis, makes her take up data management work. When a student disagrees with the system's recommendation, it means the suspension of the supply of educational resources. So, can the student really have their own choices? 
On the other hand, families with better economic conditions can privately customize more advanced AI tutors and file optimization plans. However, the opposite is true for students from ordinary families; they cannot receive better assistance.

Final page - Reflection
The development of LearnScore is aimed at ensuring that every student receives fair treatment. This system is designed to enable students' potential to be discovered without being tested by exams, and also to teach different students in many personalized ways.
However, when all actions are recorded as data, learning may be a depicted performance.
Students might pretend to be good people specifically to get higher scores and then help others. They might help others in order to protect their scores. These students may have their lifelong files blocked due to a single mistake, which will make them reluctant to try innovative things.
The system can measure attention, but it cannot understand fatigue.
The system can measure care behaviors, but it cannot understand love.
The system can measure creativity but cannot understand imagination.
The real question is not whether technology should enter education.
The real question is: To what extent should technology have the power to determine children's future?
The final question
What exactly should education protect: measurable potential or the freedom to become an unexpected person?
